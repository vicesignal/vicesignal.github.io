Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy covers Vice Signal Uploader ("the app"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Vice Signal YouTube channel. The app is used only by the channel owner to manage their own channel.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What data the app accesses

With the account holder's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses only the authorizing user's own YouTube channel to:

  • upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and publishing schedule;
  • post comments on the channel's own videos;
  • read the channel's own video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, likes, subscribers gained).

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to measure their performance.
  • OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored locally on the channel owner's computer. They are not uploaded to any other server.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored analytics data is kept while the channel is active and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page, after which the app can no longer access the account.
